# Results

One page per main result of the repository, with the precise statement, its scope and hypotheses, the Lean names, a short proof outline and references. Unless a page names a hypothesis explicitly, every theorem cited is checked by the Lean kernel with only the standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`) and depends on no unproved input.

## Main results

| Page | Result |
|---|---|
| [Siegel's theorem over totally real fields](siegel-totally-real.md) | the genus average of representation numbers of totally positive definite quadratic lattices over <img src="../images/inline/42d5ddede335.svg" align="absmiddle" alt="\mathcal{O}_F"> equals a product of local densities, every rank and every genus |
| [Tamagawa number and mass formula](tamagawa-mass-formula.md) | $`\tau(\mathrm{SO}(Q)) = 2`$ and the Smith–Minkowski–Siegel mass formula over every totally real number field |
| [Hilbert reciprocity](hilbert-reciprocity.md) | the product of the local Hilbert symbols is $`1`$ over every number field, via the Weil index |
| [Hasse–Minkowski over number fields](hasse-minkowski-number-fields.md) | the local–global principle for isotropy, representation and isometry |
| [Hasse–Minkowski over ℚ](hasse-minkowski-rationals.md) | isotropy, isometry, and the classification of forms over <img src="../images/inline/903326f5e2d2.svg" align="absmiddle" alt="\mathbb{Q}_p"> |

## Further results

| Page | Result |
|---|---|
| [Witt's theorems](witt.md) | extension, cancellation, decomposition and transitivity over every field, characteristic 2 included (Tau Ceti has versions for fields in which 2 is invertible) |

Conventions: classes of lattices are isometry classes under the full orthogonal group (improper isometries included), and each class is weighted by the inverse of the order of its orthogonal group. Bibliographic entries are in [references.bib](../../references.bib); locators marked "[unverified locator]" have not been checked against the source.
