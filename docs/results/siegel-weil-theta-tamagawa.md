matching `Solution_<book>.lean` from the library theorem named here, with only the standard axioms (`propext`,

The Siegel–Weil formula over ℚ is the case $`F = \mathbb Q`$ of the library's formula over every totally real number
field and every class number (`HilbertModular.thetaGen_eq_eisQ_uncond`, `SiegelWeil.siegelWeil_formula_lattice_uncond`),
proved by comparing all Fourier coefficients with Siegel's theorem. Theorem 10.5 of Shimura is proved by one argument
at every place, dyadic ones included: symmetries and Eichler transformations bring a vector to a normal form in a
hyperbolic splitting of the maximal lattice. Venkov's root sums follow from the vanishing of the cusp forms of weight
6, 10 and 14 for $`\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)`$ (Mathlib's dimension formulas). The commutation of the Hecke operators
is a bijection between chains of neighbours $`L - L' - M`$ for the primes $`p`$, $`q`$ in either order.
